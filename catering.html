Common Questions

Do you deliver and set up?

Yes. We can drop off, set up buffet-style, or coordinate with your venue's requirements. Just tell us what works.

What about dietary restrictions?

We work with you on modifications. Gluten-free, less sodium, extra sides—just ask upfront.

Is this the same food as the restaurant?

Exactly. Same marinade. Same grill. Same Adam. Catering doesn't get a "different recipe."

How do payments work?

Deposit to lock the date, balance due before or day-of. Clear terms. No surprises.

What's the minimum order?

Small events (under 25): No minimum. Medium events (25–100): Custom minimum. Large events (100+): Custom quote, no pressure.

How far in advance should I book?

The sooner, the better! We recommend at least 1-2 weeks for small events and 3-4 weeks for large gatherings.
